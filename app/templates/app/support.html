❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a loan online?
You can apply for a loan by visiting our Apply for Loan page, filling out the application form with your details, and submitting it. Our team will review your application and get back to you within 2-3 business days.
What documents do I need for loan application?
Required documents include: Identity proof (Aadhar, PAN), Address proof, Income proof (salary slips, bank statements), Employment certificate, and other documents as per loan type. Specific requirements will be communicated after application submission.
How can I check my loan application status?
You can check your loan application status by logging into your account and visiting the "My Applications" section. You'll also receive SMS and email updates about your application status.
What is the minimum credit score required?
We require a minimum credit score of 650 for most loan products. However, some loan types may have different requirements. Our loan officers will guide you based on your specific situation.
Can I prepay my loan without charges?
Yes, most of our loan products allow prepayment without any charges. However, some loan types may have specific terms. Please check your loan agreement or contact our support team for details.
How do I update my contact information?
You can update your contact information by visiting the Account Services page and selecting "Update Profile". You can also visit any of our branches with proper identification documents.
